## Follow-ups (manual judgement required — not auto-fixed)

These are quality nits, not bugs. Logged for next manual pass.

1. **Titles > 60 chars (136 pages, mostly auto-generated careers /
   compare / news templates).** Will get truncated in SERPs. Worth a
   pass over the template generators (`scripts/gen-careers-*.js` etc.)
   to enforce a 60-char title budget at generation time rather than
   trimming each page by hand. Examples:
   - `careers/oscp-worth-it-2026/index.html` (80c)
   - `careers/cloud-cert-salary-trends-2026/index.html` (83c)
   - `careers/devops-sre/index.html` (matching pattern)
2. **Descriptions > 160 chars (67 pages).** Mostly the same generator
   templates. `careers/oscp-worth-it-2026/index.html` is the worst at
   186c. Same recommendation: enforce 160-char cap at template-gen time
   so all future careers/compare/news pages stay in budget.
3. **OG / canonical missing on internal-only pages (acceptable).**
   `profile.html`, `reset-password.html`, `404.html`, `stats.html` skip
   OG / canonical. All are user-gated, dynamic, or carry `noindex`. Left
   as-is; flagging only.
4. **Brief is out of date for content counts.** Project brief in
   `CLAUDE.md` mentions 60 packs / 12 devstack / 9 prompts / 11 compare;
   actuals are 105 / 14 / 12 / 20. (Flagged last week too — still worth
   a docs sweep next pass.)
