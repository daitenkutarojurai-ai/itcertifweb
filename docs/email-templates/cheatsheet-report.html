Question 1 · Medium
A company runs a stateless web app on EC2 behind an Application Load Balancer. Traffic doubles every Friday at 6 PM and drops by Monday. Which scaling strategy is the most cost-effective?
A. Provision for peak Friday traffic 24/7.
 
B. Use a Spot fleet for all instances. ✗ Your answer
 
C. Use a scheduled scaling action that ramps capacity Friday at 5 PM and ramps down Monday at 9 AM. ✓ Correct
 
D. Switch to a single very large instance type.
Why
Scheduled scaling fits a known, recurring traffic pattern. Spot fleets save money but can be reclaimed mid-event. Provisioning for peak 24/7 is the most expensive option. The trick: the question gives you the timing — that’s the cue for scheduled, not target tracking.
