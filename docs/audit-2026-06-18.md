## Follow-ups (manual judgement required — not auto-fixed)

These are quality nits, not bugs. Logged for next manual pass.

1. **Titles > 60 chars (4 pages).** Will get truncated in SERPs.
   - `train.html` (66c) — `Start Training — Free IT Certification Practice Tests | CertQuests`
   - `cheatsheets/index.html` (65c) — `IT Cheatsheets — Linux, AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, Git · CertQuests`
   - `career-path/index.html` (61c) — `Career Path — Génère ta roadmap IT personnalisée · CertQuests`
   - `careers/index.html` (82c) — `IT Career Roadmaps 2026: 6 Paths, Cert ROI & Interview Prep — CertQuests`
2. **Description > 160 chars (1 page).** `certifications/paloalto.html`
   description is 163c — trim 3 characters to fit Google snippet width.
3. **Missing OG / canonical on internal-only pages (acceptable).**
   `profile.html`, `reset-password.html`, `404.html`, `stats.html` skip
   OG / canonical tags. All four are either user-gated, dynamic, or
   carry `noindex` already — no SEO impact. Left as-is; flagging only.
4. **Above-the-fold images without `loading="lazy"`.** 8 pages
   (`index.html`, several `certifications/*.html`, `careers/index.html`,
   `compare/index.html`). These are mostly hero/logo images that are
   visible immediately, so lazy-loading would actively hurt LCP. Leave
   as-is unless individual images are confirmed below-the-fold.
5. **Cache-buster discipline.** Single source of truth still works —
   one stale page slipped through this week. Worth keeping the
   one-liner from `CLAUDE.md` "Before committing" routine handy:
   `grep -rlE '\.(css|js)\?v=N' --include="*.html" .`
6. **Brief is out of date for content counts.** Project brief in
   `CLAUDE.md` mentions 60 packs / 12 devstack / 9 prompts / 11 compare;
   actuals are 102 / 14 / 11 / 20. Worth a docs sweep next pass.
