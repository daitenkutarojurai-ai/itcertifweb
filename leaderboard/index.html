One board, web + app

Rankings come from the same Supabase functions the CertQuests Android app uses, so a player grinding on their phone and one studying in the browser appear side by side. Sign in with the same account on either and your XP follows you.

All-time vs this week

The all-time tab ranks by lifetime XP. The this week tab ranks by XP earned in the current ISO week (Monday → Sunday, UTC) and resets every Monday — a fresh sprint for regular learners.

Why so few players (or zero)?

The leaderboard is opt-in only. Most players never flip the switch. If the table is empty, it just means nobody's published their score yet — go to your profile and become the first.

RGPD

Only your display name (separate from your auth username if you set one) and XP appear. No email, no user_id, no contact info. Flip the toggle off anytime and you're gone from the next page load.
