Who is each one for?

AWS Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03)

Amazon AWS · SAA-C03
You're targeting Cloud Engineer / Solutions Architect roles at a tech-native company or cloud consultancy. The broadest job market.
  • The largest cloud market in France and globally (~32% of the public cloud market in 2025).
  • Senior median salary of €65k in France (Hays/APEC 2026).
  • The most mature training ecosystem: ACG, Stéphane Maarek, Tutorials Dojo.
  • Recognized at virtually every tech-native company and cloud consultancy.
  • A demanding technical exam — 65 questions, many complex scenarios.
  • Takes ~120 hours of prep with hands-on practice; without AWS Free Tier labs, failure is likely.
  • More 'architect' focused (trade-offs) than admin focused (day-to-day operations).

Microsoft Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104)

Microsoft Azure · AZ-104
You're targeting a traditional enterprise (banking, insurance, manufacturing, public sector) or you come from a Windows/sysadmin background.
  • A very strong Azure market in banking, insurance and the public sector (Microsoft's legacy footprint).
  • Senior median salary of €60k in France, €78k in Luxembourg (Hays 2026).
  • More focused on day-to-day operations (deploy, configure, monitor) — practical for sysadmins changing careers.
  • Microsoft Learn (official) is free and excellent — no paid bootcamp needed.
  • Valued less than the AWS cert at tech-native startups and scale-ups.
  • The Microsoft core (Active Directory, Entra ID) transfers less well outside Azure.
  • $165 exam, plus the Microsoft ecosystem (PowerShell + Azure CLI) to learn if you're not familiar with it.
