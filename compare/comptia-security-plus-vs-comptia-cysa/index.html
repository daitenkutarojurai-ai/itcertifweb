Who is each one for?

CompTIA Security+

CompTIA · SY0-701
You're new to cybersecurity — it's the first cert to put on your résumé.
  • The most requested entry-level security cert in junior job postings.
  • Covers the whole foundation: threats, cryptography, IAM, architecture, governance.
  • A DoD 8570/8140 baseline — the key to many US security contracts.
  • An implicit prerequisite for nearly everything else in cyber.
  • $404 — the exam isn't cheap.
  • Broad but shallow: it doesn't prove analyst skills.
  • Mixes multiple choice and performance-based questions (PBQs) — don't underestimate the prep.

CompTIA CySA+

CompTIA · CS0-003
You already have the basics (or Security+) and are targeting a SOC analyst role.
  • Blue-team focused: detection, threat hunting, analytics, MITRE ATT&CK.
  • The CompTIA cert closest to a SOC analyst's real day-to-day.
  • Recognized for analyst / tier-1 and tier-2 SOC roles.
  • The logical follow-up to Security+ in the CompTIA pathway.
  • Assumes you already have the Security+ fundamentals.
  • Too specialized to serve as your very first cert.
  • Same price as Security+ ($404) for a narrower audience.
