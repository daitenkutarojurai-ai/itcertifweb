Who is each one for?

Red Hat Certified System Administrator

Red Hat · EX200
You're targeting an enterprise sysadmin / ops role — the RHCSA is the real employer signal.
  • A 100% hands-on exam — it proves real skill, not memorization.
  • The Linux cert most respected by enterprise recruiters.
  • RHEL dominates enterprise Linux — the cert lands squarely on the market.
  • A direct springboard to the RHCE and a real Red Hat career.
  • $500 — the more expensive of the two.
  • Centered on Red Hat / RHEL and its toolchain (dnf, SELinux, systemd).
  • A practical exam under pressure — no multiple choice to fall back on.

CompTIA Linux+

CompTIA · XK0-005
You're new to Linux and want a first vendor-neutral cert before tackling the hands-on RHCSA.
  • Vendor-neutral — concepts that carry over to Debian / Ubuntu / RHEL.
  • A mix of multiple choice and performance-based questions: more predictable prep.
  • A good warm-up before investing in the hands-on RHCSA.
  • $369 — a bit cheaper than the Red Hat exam.
  • Carries less weight with employers than the RHCSA in interviews.
  • Doesn't prove hands-on skill as strongly as a 100% practical exam.
  • $369 is still a significant budget for a warm-up cert.
