Who is each one for?

CompTIA Security+

CompTIA · SY0-701
You're starting out in security, you don't have 5 years of experience, and you want to land your first job.
  • The standard entry-level cert — recognized by virtually every junior security recruiter.
  • No experience prerequisite to sit the exam.
  • 90-minute exam, mostly multiple choice — predictable preparation.
  • A DoD 8570/8140 baseline — required for many US government / contractor roles.
  • A natural bridge to CySA+, PenTest+, and later the CISSP.
  • A low ceiling — few employers go much beyond entry-level pay on Security+ alone (rarely above €50k in France).
  • Breadth over depth: covers everything, masters nothing in depth.
  • Less recognized in Europe than in the US.

Certified Information Systems Security Professional

ISC² · CISSP
You have 4-5 years of security/IT experience and are targeting a senior role (CISO, security architect, audit).
  • The most recognized security cert worldwide.
  • Senior median salary of €80-95k in France, €110-130k in Switzerland/Luxembourg (Hays 2026).
  • Opens the door to CISO and Security Architect roles.
  • International recognition — helps with mobility to the US/UK/Canada.
  • 5 years of documented security experience required for the full cert (without it, you become an 'Associate of ISC2').
  • $749 for the exam + a $125 annual maintenance fee.
  • 3-hour adaptive exam, 100-150 questions — 300-500 hours of prep if you're starting from a thin base.
  • Covers 8 very broad domains — not a hyper-technical, hands-on cert.
