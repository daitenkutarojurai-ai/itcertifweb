Who is each one for?

AWS Solutions Architect Associate

Amazon AWS · SAA-C03
You want the safest bet on the job market. If you're undecided, this is it.
  • The largest cloud job market in France and across Europe.
  • Senior median salary of €65k in France (Hays/APEC 2026).
  • A huge ecosystem of courses, community and books.
  • Recognized by virtually every cloud recruiter.
  • A technical exam — not for a complete beginner.
  • $150 for the exam, ~120 hours of study.
  • Some questions take 2-3 years of experience to grasp the nuance.

Azure Administrator Associate

Microsoft · AZ-104
You come from a Windows/.NET environment, or you're targeting banking, consulting or the public sector.
  • Best ROI if your company already runs on Microsoft (Office 365, Intune, AD).
  • More admin/ops-oriented than architecture-oriented — familiar ground for a sysadmin.
  • Strong demand in the public sector, banking and large enterprises.
  • A smaller market than AWS outside banking and the public sector.
  • Salaries typically 5-10% below AWS SAA for an equivalent role.
  • A sprawling exam — it covers 6 very broad domains.

Google Associate Cloud Engineer

Google Cloud · ACE
You're targeting startups, data or ML AND you live in a major tech hub.
  • The most approachable exam of the three — more direct questions.
  • GCP is concentrated in tech companies, startups and data/ML.
  • A salary premium for data/ML profiles.
  • $125 — the cheapest of the three.
  • A smaller GCP job market (strong in major tech hubs, limited elsewhere).
  • Fewer open roles — a longer job search.
  • Less recognition outside the tech/startup ecosystem.
