Who is each one for?

Cisco Certified Network Associate

Cisco · 200-301
You want a real networking career (NOC, network engineer, ISP, carrier).
  • The #1 recognized credential in networking — big carriers, IT services firms, banks.
  • A technically deep exam (routing, switching, security, automation).
  • Senior median salary of €55k in France (APEC 2026).
  • Prepares you for the CCNP — a real Cisco career path afterward.
  • $300 for the exam, ~200 hours of study — a heavy investment.
  • Very Cisco IOS/NX-OS oriented — less universal than people claim.
  • Includes labs/simulations — not just multiple choice.

CompTIA Network+

CompTIA · N10-009
You're new to networking and want to test your interest before investing 200 hours in the CCNA.
  • Vendor-neutral — concepts that carry over to Cisco / Juniper / Arista / Aruba.
  • A more predictable exam — mostly multiple choice.
  • Recognized for US government / DoD work (8570/8140 baseline).
  • An excellent prelude to the CCNA — you learn the concepts before the Cisco syntax.
  • Limited recognition outside the US and the defense sector.
  • $369 — paradoxically more expensive than the CCNA.
  • Won't carry you to a senior networking career on its own — you'll need to follow up with the CCNA/CCNP.
