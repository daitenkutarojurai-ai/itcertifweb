Who is each one for?

AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner

Amazon AWS · CLF-C02
You want a broad cloud career or plan to freelance — AWS remains the safest bet.
  • AWS has the largest pool of cloud job openings — the broadest bet.
  • Recognized everywhere: IT services firms, consulting, freelance, large enterprises.
  • A well-marked path to the SAA-C03 next, with a huge amount of free learning material.
  • A $100 exam — the cheapest entry ticket of the three.
  • A purely conceptual multiple-choice exam — it proves no hands-on skill.
  • AWS is losing some ground to Azure in large enterprises.
  • Still ~40 hours of study for a 'fundamentals' exam.

Microsoft Azure Fundamentals

Microsoft Azure · AZ-900
Your employer or target is a bank, a government agency or a large corporation — Azure rules there.
  • Azure dominates large enterprises, banks and the public sector.
  • Natural fit with Microsoft 365, which is already everywhere in corporate IT.
  • An approachable exam — a direct stepping stone to the AZ-104 Administrator.
  • Instant internal recognition if your employer is a Microsoft shop.
  • A fast-moving Azure ecosystem — services get renamed frequently.
  • Mostly valuable if your employer (or target employer) is already on Azure.
  • Like the CLF, it is conceptual — no proof of hands-on practice.

Google Cloud Digital Leader

Google Cloud · Cloud Digital Leader
You work in a non-technical role (sales, pre-sales, project management) or you are targeting a data-driven company or GCP startup.
  • Designed for non-technical profiles — business vocabulary and use cases.
  • GCP is strong in data / ML and among tech startups.
  • A good foundation for working with cloud teams without being an engineer.
  • An approachable exam, focused on decisions rather than configuration.
  • GCP has a smaller market share — fewer job openings.
  • Not a direct technical stepping stone (unlike CLF → SAA).
  • Rarely treated as a 'technical résumé' cert by recruiters.
