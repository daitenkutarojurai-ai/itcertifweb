Why an Okta certification?

Okta is the leading independent identity and access management (IAM) platform, used by enterprises to secure single sign-on, multi-factor authentication and user lifecycle across thousands of applications. The Okta Certified Professional credential proves you understand core identity concepts and can perform day-to-day administration of the Okta platform — skills that translate directly into help desk, IT administrator and identity engineer roles.

CertQuests covers the Okta Certified Professional exam objectives with realistic IAM scenarios and clear explanations.

Okta Certified Professional exam topics

  • Identity and access management fundamentals
  • Single sign-on (SSO) and application integration
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) and authentication policies
  • Universal Directory, users and groups
  • Lifecycle management and provisioning
  • Self-service registration and password policies

Frequently asked questions

Is the Okta practice test free?
Yes. Free Okta Certified Professional questions covering identity and access management, no signup required.
What does the Okta Certified Professional exam cover?
It covers core Okta concepts: single sign-on (SSO), multi-factor authentication (MFA), Universal Directory, lifecycle management, user and group provisioning, and basic application integration.
Who is the Okta Certified Professional for?
It is aimed at help desk staff, IT administrators and identity newcomers who manage day-to-day Okta tasks. It is the entry-level credential before the Administrator and Consultant exams.
What is the format of the Okta Certified Professional exam?
It is a multiple-choice, proctored online exam focused on practical identity and access management scenarios within the Okta platform.
