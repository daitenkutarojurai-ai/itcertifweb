Why Fortinet NSE?

Fortinet is one of the top three firewall vendors and Fortinet-certified engineers are in short supply. NSE 4 is the role-based certification for FortiGate operators and the most valuable Fortinet credential in the market. NSE 5 and NSE 7 build on NSE 4 toward senior security engineer roles.

CertQuests' Fortinet pack has 170+ NSE 4 questions covering firewall policies, routing, VPNs, high availability, SD-WAN and FortiOS administration.

NSE 4 exam topics

  • System and network settings
  • Firewall policies and NAT
  • Authentication and identity
  • SSL VPN and IPsec VPN
  • Intrusion Prevention, antivirus, web filtering
  • High availability and SD-WAN

Study tips

Hands-on experience on a FortiGate (physical or VM) makes a huge difference. Combine CertQuests with lab work on FortiGate-VM.

Frequently asked questions

How many NSE 4 practice questions are included?
170+ free questions targeting the current NSE 4 FortiGate Security exam blueprint.
Is NSE 5 or NSE 7 available?
They are on the roadmap. Start with NSE 4 — it is the prerequisite for most senior Fortinet roles anyway.
Do I need a FortiGate lab to pass NSE 4?
Strongly recommended. Fortinet offers free FortiGate-VM trials you can spin up on ESXi, KVM or AWS.
How long does NSE certification last?
Fortinet NSE certifications expire every 2 years and require recertification to stay valid.
