Why the Databricks Data Engineer Associate certification?

Databricks sits at the center of the modern data stack, and the Data Engineer Associate credential proves you can do introductory data engineering on its Lakehouse platform — using Delta Lake correctly, building ELT with Spark SQL and Python, processing data incrementally, and putting pipelines into production with basic governance.

Demand for lakehouse skills has grown with the platform itself, and this associate exam is the standard entry point before the Professional tier. CertQuests covers the blueprint with realistic notebook, pipeline and Delta scenarios and detailed explanations.

Data Engineer Associate exam domains

  • Databricks Lakehouse Platform (workspace, clusters, notebooks, Delta Lake)
  • ELT with Spark SQL and Python
  • Incremental Data Processing (Auto Loader, Structured Streaming)
  • Production Pipelines (jobs, Delta Live Tables)
  • Data Governance (Unity Catalog, permissions)

Getting hands-on

Frequently asked questions

What is the Databricks Data Engineer Associate certification?
The Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate validates introductory data-engineering skills on the Databricks Lakehouse platform: Delta Lake fundamentals, ELT with Spark SQL and Python, incremental data processing, production pipelines and basic data governance with Unity Catalog.
What is the Databricks Data Engineer Associate exam format and cost?
The exam has 45 multiple-choice questions in 90 minutes, delivered as an online proctored test for USD 200. There are no formal prerequisites, though Databricks recommends about six months of hands-on experience.
Who should take the Data Engineer Associate certification?
Data engineers, analytics engineers and BI developers who build on Databricks — or engineers moving from a warehouse-only stack (Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift) who want to prove lakehouse skills.
Are these Databricks practice questions free?
Yes. The CertQuests Databricks Data Engineer Associate practice questions are completely free with explanations, and no signup is required.
