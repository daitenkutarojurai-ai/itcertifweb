Why F5 certifications?

F5 BIG-IP sits in the data path of a huge share of enterprise applications, terminating connections, balancing traffic and offloading SSL. The F5 Certified BIG-IP Administrator (F5-CA) credential — earned by passing exams 101 and 201 — proves you can deploy, operate and troubleshoot TMOS in production, a skill set that maps directly to network, systems and application-delivery engineering roles.

CertQuests covers the 201 TMOS Administration objectives with practical traffic-management and troubleshooting scenarios.

201 TMOS Administration exam topics

  • TMOS architecture and the packet/connection flow through the BIG-IP
  • Virtual servers, profiles and load-balancing methods
  • Pools, pool members and health monitors
  • SNAT, NAT and source/destination address translation
  • Persistence (cookie, source-address) and SSL offload
  • High availability, failover and config sync
  • Administration and troubleshooting with the GUI, tmsh and logs

Frequently asked questions

What does the F5 201 TMOS Administration exam cover?
Exam 201 covers TMOS architecture and traffic flow, virtual servers and profiles, load-balancing methods, pool members and health monitors, SNAT, persistence, SSL offload, high-availability failover, and administration via the GUI, tmsh and logs.
Who is the F5 BIG-IP Administrator certification for?
It's aimed at network, systems and application-delivery engineers who deploy and operate F5 BIG-IP. Passing exams 101 and 201 earns the F5 Certified BIG-IP Administrator (F5-CA) credential.
Is the F5 practice test free?
Yes. 12 free F5 BIG-IP Administrator (201 TMOS) questions with explanations, no signup required.
What format is the F5 201 exam?
F5 exam 201 is an online, proctored, multiple-choice test focused on administering and troubleshooting BIG-IP running TMOS.
