Why AWS certifications matter

AWS is the #1 cloud platform by market share, and certified AWS professionals command some of the highest salaries in IT. The Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) is the entry point — a foundational exam that proves you understand AWS core services, pricing and the shared-responsibility model. From there, the Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) is the most in-demand certification for cloud engineers.

CertQuests' AWS question bank covers the 2025 exam blueprint and includes scenario-based questions, not just trivia. Both the CLF-C02 and SAA-C03 packs are live — no signup required.

Study strategy

Do the full-length practice exam under timed conditions first to benchmark yourself. Review every wrong answer, then focus study mode on the weak domains. Retake the full exam 48 hours before the real one. For SAA-C03, pay special attention to scenario-based questions — the exam tests judgment, not memorisation.
