Why AWS certifications matter

AWS is the #1 cloud platform by market share, and certified AWS professionals command some of the highest salaries in IT. The Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) is the entry point — a foundational exam that proves you understand AWS core services, pricing and the shared-responsibility model. From there, the Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03) is the most in-demand certification for cloud engineers.
