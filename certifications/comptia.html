The CompTIA certification ladder

CompTIA certifications are vendor-neutral, making them ideal for IT professionals who want broad skills rather than vendor lock-in. The classic progression is A+ → Network+ → Security+, the trifecta most help-desk and SOC hiring managers look for.

CertQuests covers the current exam versions: A+ 220-1101/1102, Network+ N10-009 and Security+ SY0-701.

What's unique about CompTIA exams?

  • Performance-based questions (PBQs) that simulate real tasks
  • Multiple-choice with more than one correct answer
  • Scenario-heavy format — context matters
  • Passing score is a scaled score, not a raw percentage

How to study efficiently

Rotate Quick Quiz sessions during short breaks, do Study Mode when you're at your desk, and simulate Full Exam under timer conditions twice in the week before your test date.
