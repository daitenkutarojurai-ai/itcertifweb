Palo Alto Networks leads the next-generation firewall market and PCNSA/PCNSE holders are in high demand at enterprises and MSPs. PCNSA (Associate) is the entry-level cert; PCNSE (Engineer) is the senior-level exam that commands premium salaries.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Palo Alto PCNSA pack free?
Yes. 50+ questions covering the current PCNSA exam blueprint, free with no signup.
PCNSA or PCNSE — which should I take?
PCNSA if you have less than 1 year of Palo Alto experience. PCNSE if you administer PAN-OS daily.
Does PCNSA expire?
Yes, Palo Alto certifications expire 2 years after earning them.
What PAN-OS version do these questions target?
The current PCNSA blueprint is based on PAN-OS 10+ concepts.
