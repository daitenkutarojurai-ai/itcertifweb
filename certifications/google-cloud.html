Why Google Cloud certifications?

Google Cloud (GCP) is one of the big three hyperscalers, and its certifications are among the most respected in the industry — the Professional Cloud Architect has repeatedly ranked as one of the highest-paying IT certifications. GCP skills are in demand for data, machine learning, Kubernetes and modern cloud-native engineering roles.

CertQuests covers the full GCP ladder with real exam-style scenarios and explanations, so you can move from foundational knowledge to advanced architecture and specialty tracks.

The Google Cloud certification ladder

  • Cloud Digital Leader — foundational, business-oriented; no hands-on required
  • Associate Cloud Engineer (ACE) — the core hands-on cert for deploying and operating GCP
  • Professional Cloud Architect (PCA) — design secure, scalable, reliable solutions
  • Professional Data Engineer (PDE) — build data pipelines and ML-ready systems on GCP
  • Professional Cloud Security Engineer — secure workloads, IAM and compliance
  • Professional Cloud Network Engineer — design and operate GCP networking

Who these certs are for

The Cloud Digital Leader suits product managers, sales engineers and anyone needing cloud fluency. The Associate Cloud Engineer is the right starting point for engineers and admins. The Professional tracks target architects, data engineers, security specialists and network engineers who already have hands-on GCP experience.

Frequently asked questions

Which Google Cloud certification should I take first?
Most people start with the Associate Cloud Engineer (ACE), which is the foundational hands-on cert. If you are non-technical or in a business role, the Cloud Digital Leader is a gentler entry point before moving to the Professional tracks.
What is the difference between the Associate Cloud Engineer and the Professional Cloud Architect?
The Associate Cloud Engineer (ACE) focuses on deploying and operating GCP resources day to day. The Professional Cloud Architect (PCA) is harder and design-focused — it tests your ability to architect secure, scalable, reliable solutions and is considered an advanced exam.
Are these Google Cloud practice tests free?
Yes. All CertQuests GCP practice questions are 100% free with explanations, and no signup is required to start.
How much do Google Cloud exams cost and what is the format?
The Associate Cloud Engineer exam costs about $125 USD and the Professional exams cost about $200 USD. They are multiple-choice and multiple-select, run for around 2 hours, and can be taken online with remote proctoring or at a test center.
How long is a Google Cloud certification valid?
Associate and Professional certifications are valid for 2 years; the Cloud Digital Leader is valid for 3 years. You renew by re-taking the current version of the exam.
