Frequently Asked Questions

What is the GCP Professional Data Engineer exam?

The PDE exam (updated 2023) validates your ability to design and build data processing systems, ingest and process data with BigQuery and Dataflow, store data in the right GCP service for the workload, prepare data for analysis and ML, and maintain automated data pipelines. It covers 5 domains over ~2 hours with 50–60 questions.

How is the GCP PDE different from the GCP PCA?

The PCA focuses on overall cloud architecture — compute, networking, security, and SRE practices. The PDE specializes in the data layer: BigQuery, Dataflow, Pub/Sub, Bigtable, Cloud Composer, Vertex AI, and data governance. Many engineers pursue both as complements. If your role is data or ML engineering, start with PDE.

What GCP services are most important for the PDE exam?

BigQuery and Dataflow are the two most heavily tested services. You also need deep knowledge of: Pub/Sub (ordering, dead-letter, exactly-once), Bigtable (row key design, hot spots), Cloud Spanner (interleaving, commit timestamps), Dataproc (ephemeral clusters, autoscaling), Cloud Composer (DAGs, XCom), Vertex AI (Feature Store, Pipelines, AutoML), Cloud DLP (de-identification), and Dataplex (data governance zones).

How should I prepare for the GCP PDE exam?

Build hands-on experience with BigQuery, Dataflow, and Pub/Sub first. Study the official GCP documentation for each service's use cases and limitations. Practice with scenario-based questions that test service selection (e.g., Bigtable vs BigQuery for time-series data). Our free 60-question practice test and study course cover all 5 exam domains.
