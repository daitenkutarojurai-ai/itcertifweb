Why the Juniper JNCIA-Junos certification?

JNCIA-Junos is the entry point to Juniper's certification track and the foundation for the JNCIS, JNCIP and JNCIE tiers. It proves you understand core networking and how to operate, configure and monitor devices running the Junos OS — skills that matter in any service-provider or enterprise network built on Juniper gear.

For network engineers who already know Cisco IOS, JNCIA-Junos is a fast, high-value way to prove cross-vendor competence. CertQuests covers the JN0-105 objectives with realistic CLI and routing scenarios and detailed explanations.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Juniper JNCIA-Junos certification?
JNCIA-Junos (exam JN0-105) is Juniper Networks' associate-level credential. It validates a foundational understanding of networking and of the Junos OS — its CLI, configuration model, and the routing, switching and policy features common across Juniper platforms — and is the prerequisite mindset for the rest of the Juniper certification track.
What is the JN0-105 exam format and cost?
The JNCIA-Junos exam has 65 multiple-choice questions, runs 90 minutes, and is delivered at Pearson VUE for about USD 200. There is no formal experience prerequisite, which makes it a popular first networking certification or a quick cross-vendor add-on.
Who should take the JNCIA-Junos certification?
JNCIA-Junos suits network engineers, NOC technicians and students who work with — or want to work with — Juniper routers, switches and firewalls. It is also a practical way for Cisco-trained engineers to demonstrate multi-vendor ability.

Yes. The CertQuests JNCIA-Junos practice questions are completely free with explanations, and no signup is required.
