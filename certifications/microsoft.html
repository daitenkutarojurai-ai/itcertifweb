Why Azure certifications?

Microsoft Azure is the second-largest cloud platform and the leader in hybrid-cloud deployments. The AZ-900 Azure Fundamentals exam is the fastest way to get a Microsoft certification on your résumé, followed by the AZ-104 Administrator role-based cert.

CertQuests covers all major Azure role-based exams with questions that reflect the 2025 exam blueprints.

Azure AZ-900 topics

  • Cloud concepts (25-30%)
  • Azure architecture and services (35-40%)
  • Azure management and governance (30-35%)

Azure study path

Start with AZ-900 (fundamentals) → AZ-104 (administrator) → choose a specialty: AZ-305 (architect), AZ-500 (security) or AZ-700 (network).

Frequently asked questions

Is AZ-900 really worth taking?
Yes. AZ-900 is the most popular entry point to Microsoft cloud certifications and is often required or recommended for IT support roles, sales engineering and architecture candidates.
How hard is AZ-104 compared to AZ-900?
AZ-104 is significantly harder — it requires hands-on knowledge of VMs, networking, storage, Azure AD and monitoring. Practice exams are essential.
Are CertQuests Azure questions updated for the new exam objectives?
Yes. Questions target the current exam blueprints as of 2025.
Can I combine Azure and AWS prep here?
Absolutely. Your progress is tracked per certification, so you can study multiple clouds in parallel.
