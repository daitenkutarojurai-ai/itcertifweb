Why the ISACA CISM certification?

CISM is one of the world's most recognised credentials for information security management. It validates that you can design, oversee and assess an enterprise security program — governance, risk, program development and incident response — knowledge that maps directly to senior security roles such as CISO, security manager and head of GRC.

CISM consistently ranks among the highest-paying IT certifications in salary surveys and is frequently listed as a requirement or preferred credential for security leadership job postings. Frequently asked questions

What is the ISACA CISM certification?
CISM (Certified Information Security Manager) is ISACA's globally recognised credential for information security managers. It validates expertise across four domains: governance, risk management, security program development, and incident management — aimed at security leaders who design and oversee enterprise security programs.
What is the CISM exam format and cost?
The CISM exam has 150 multiple-choice questions, runs 4 hours, and requires a 450/800 scaled passing score. It costs about USD 575 for ISACA members or USD 760 for non-members. Five years of relevant experience is required for full certification, with substitutions available.
Who should take the CISM certification?
CISM is aimed at experienced security managers, CISOs and IT leaders responsible for governance, risk and security program oversight. It is one of the highest-paying credentials in the industry and is often required for senior security-leadership roles.
