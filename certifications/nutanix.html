Why the Nutanix NCP-MCI certification?

Hyperconverged infrastructure collapses compute, storage and virtualization into one cluster, and NCP-MCI is the credential that proves you can run one. It covers the pieces an administrator actually touches: AHV virtual machines and networking, Prism Element for a single cluster and Prism Central across many, storage containers on the Distributed Storage Fabric, and the Controller VM that serves I/O on every node.

The demand is downstream of the VMware licensing upheaval — organizations re-evaluating vSphere estates look at AHV, and they need administrators who can operate it rather than only demo it. CertQuests covers the NCP-MCI-6.5 objectives with operator-lens scenarios and detailed explanations.

NCP-MCI exam topics

  • Cluster architecture — nodes, blocks, the Controller VM and AOS services
  • Storage — containers, the Distributed Storage Fabric, deduplication, compression and erasure coding
  • AHV — VM lifecycle, virtual networking, affinity rules and live migration
  • Resiliency — redundancy factor, replication factor and failure domains
  • Data protection — snapshots, protection domains, replication and recovery
  • Operations — Prism dashboards, alerts, capacity runway and LCM upgrades

Getting hands-on

Pair CertQuests practice with Nutanix Test Drive or a Community Edition cluster. The exam rewards administrators who have actually set a redundancy factor, watched a node fail over, and run an LCM upgrade — not just memorized the feature list.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Nutanix NCP-MCI certification?
NCP-MCI (Nutanix Certified Professional — Multicloud Infrastructure) validates that you can deploy, administer and troubleshoot a Nutanix cluster: AHV virtualization, Prism Element and Prism Central, the Distributed Storage Fabric, Controller VMs, storage containers, data protection and cluster lifecycle operations. The 6.5 track targets AOS 6.5.
Which topics does the NCP-MCI-6.5 exam cover?
Cluster deployment and configuration, AHV networking and VM management, storage containers and the Distributed Storage Fabric, data resiliency and redundancy factor, backup and disaster recovery, monitoring and alerting in Prism, capacity planning, lifecycle upgrades with LCM, and day-to-day troubleshooting from the CVM.
Who should take the NCP-MCI certification?
Infrastructure and virtualization administrators who run hyperconverged clusters, VMware administrators evaluating or migrating to AHV, and consultants who deploy Nutanix in enterprise data centres.
