Frequently Asked Questions

Is CertQuest really free?
Yes. CertQuest offers 1,500+ practice questions across 40+ IT certifications completely free. All quiz modes, progress tracking, and gamification features are included at no cost.
What IT certifications does CertQuest cover?
CertQuest covers certifications from 10 major vendors: Cisco (CCNA, CCNP), Amazon AWS (Cloud Practitioner, Solutions Architect), Microsoft Azure (AZ-900, AZ-104), CompTIA (A+, Network+, Security+, CySA+), Fortinet (NSE4, NSE5), Palo Alto Networks (PCNSA, PCNSE), Red Hat (RHCSA, RHCE), ServiceNow (CSA), Splunk (Core, Enterprise), and Linux/DevOps tools (Terraform, Vault, Kubernetes, Docker).
How many practice questions are available?
CertQuest currently has over 1,500 practice questions across all available certification packs, with more being added regularly. Each certification pack contains between 50 and 170 questions covering all exam domains.
Do I need to create an account?
No. CertQuest requires no account, login, or registration. All your progress, scores, and achievements are stored locally on your device. Your data never leaves your device.
What quiz modes are available?
CertQuest offers three quiz modes: Quick Quiz (5 random questions in ~2 minutes), Full Exam (all questions with a 30-second timer simulating real exam conditions), and Study Mode (10 questions with no timer and explanations always shown).
Can I use CertQuest offline?
Yes. Once question packs have been loaded, CertQuest works fully offline. The app stores everything locally so you can study anywhere without an internet connection.
Is CertQuest available on Android?
Yes. CertQuest is available as a free app on the Google Play Store. You can also use it directly in your web browser at certquest.app.
Are the questions based on real exam objectives?
Yes. All questions are aligned with official exam objectives and domains. They cover the same topics you'll encounter on the real certification exam, helping you identify knowledge gaps before test day.
