Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

Is CertQuests really free?
Yes. Training quizzes, courses and tech news are all free. No signup, no paywall, no tracking.
What's the difference between Training, Courses and News?
Training is the practice-quiz engine for exam prep. Courses are structured learning paths that walk you through a certification domain by domain. News is a curated feed of IT, cloud and security stories worth your time.
What IT certifications does CertQuests cover?
Cisco (CCNA, CCNP), Amazon AWS (Cloud Practitioner, Solutions Architect), Microsoft Azure (AZ-900, AZ-104), CompTIA (A+, Network+, Security+, CySA+), Fortinet (NSE4, NSE5), Palo Alto Networks (PCNSA, PCNSE), Red Hat (RHCSA, RHCE), ServiceNow (CSA), Splunk (Core, Enterprise), and Linux/DevOps tools (Terraform, Vault, Kubernetes, Docker).
Do I need to create an account?
No. All your progress, scores and achievements are stored locally on your device. Your data never leaves your browser.
Can I use CertQuests offline?
Yes. Once question packs are loaded, training works fully offline. The Android app also works offline end-to-end.
How often is the news feed updated?
The news feed is curated weekly with a small handful of high-signal stories — major cloud announcements, notable security incidents, certification news, and tools worth trying. No daily churn.
Are the questions based on real exam objectives?
Yes. All questions are aligned with official exam objectives and domains. They cover the same topics you'll encounter on the real certification exam.
